EC · Synthetic pyrethroid · Fast-knockdown broad-spectrum insecticide

Deltamethrin 2.8% EC (marketed as DR. DELTA) is a premium, fast-acting synthetic pyrethroid insecticide engineered for quick knockdown and dependable, broad-spectrum pest control. The emulsifiable concentrate (EC) formulation mixes cleanly, spreads evenly across foliage, and protects crops from bollworms, caterpillars, thrips, aphids, jassids, and whiteflies in cotton, chilli, vegetables, pulses, and oilseeds.

Spray timing
