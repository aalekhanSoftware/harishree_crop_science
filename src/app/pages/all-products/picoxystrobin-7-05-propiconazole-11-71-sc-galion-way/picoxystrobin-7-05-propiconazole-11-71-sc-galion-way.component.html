Dosage & application guide

Clear, field-ready guidance for using GALION WAY Fungicide effectively.

Recommended dose

30 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 30 ml GALION WAY, top up, and agitate. The SC formulation forms a uniform spray suspension.

Farmer tips for best results

Scout fields regularly, spray at early disease signs, cover leaf undersides, and avoid spraying before heavy rain or strong wind.
