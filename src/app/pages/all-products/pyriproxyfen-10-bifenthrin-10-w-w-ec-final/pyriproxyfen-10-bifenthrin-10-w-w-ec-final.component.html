Dual active ingredient technology

Pyriproxyfen 10% — insect growth regulator (IGR)

Pyriproxyfen (IRAC Group 7D) mimics juvenile hormone, preventing normal pest development and interrupting the reproductive cycle. This Pyriproxyfen insecticide mechanism reduces egg hatchability, controls immature stages, and prevents future infestations — especially effective against whiteflies, aphids, and allied sap feeders when rotated within integrated pest management programs.

Bifenthrin 10% — contact, stomach & residual action

Bifenthrin (IRAC Group 3A) delivers immediate impact through contact action — rapid paralysis of exposed pests — and stomach action when insects ingest treated tissue. The Bifenthrin insecticide component provides extended field protection through residual activity on foliage, making FINAL a true fast knockdown insecticide with lasting crop protection between spray intervals.

Pyriproxyfen 10% + Bifenthrin 10% w/w EC mode of action

How Pyriproxyfen and Bifenthrin in FINAL deliver contact, stomach, and insect growth regulation for comprehensive sucking pest control.

Contact action — rapid paralysis

Bifenthrin acts on contact, disrupting sodium channels in insect nerve tissue. Thrips, whiteflies, and aphids exposed to the spray deposit experience rapid paralysis and immediate cessation of feeding.

Stomach poison action

When sucking pests ingest treated plant tissue, Bifenthrin delivers strong stomach poison insecticide activity against hidden feeders on leaf undersides and in dense canopy.

insect growth regulation — Pyriproxyfen

Pyriproxyfen blocks juvenile hormone mimic, interrupting energy production. Susceptible pests lose the ability to feed and move normally — a powerful Pyriproxyfen insecticide mechanism.

Resistance management benefits

Rotate FINAL with neonicotinoids, IGRs, and other MOA groups across the season to reduce selection pressure on thrips, whitefly, and aphid populations.
