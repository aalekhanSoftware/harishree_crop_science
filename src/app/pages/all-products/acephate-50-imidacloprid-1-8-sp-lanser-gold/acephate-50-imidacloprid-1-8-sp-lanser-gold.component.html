Dual active ingredient technology

Acephate 50% — organophosphate insecticide

Acephate (IRAC Group 1B) inhibits acetylcholinesterase in insect nerve tissue, disrupting nervous-system function rapidly. This Acephate insecticide mechanism delivers contact action, translaminar movement through leaf cuticle, and fast pest suppression against sucking and chewing pests. Benefits include quick visible results, effective activity against whiteflies, aphids, thrips, jassids, and allied sap feeders, plus deep crop protection through translaminar penetration.

Imidacloprid 1.8% — neonicotinoid systemic action

Imidacloprid (IRAC Group 4A) binds to nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, causing feeding cessation and progressive pest mortality. The Imidacloprid insecticide component absorbs through foliage and translocates systemically via xylem and phloem, protecting new shoots and unsprayed plant parts. This makes LANSER GOLD a true systemic insecticide with long-lasting crop protection between spray intervals.

ACEPHATE 50% + IMIDACLOPRID 1.8% SP mode of action

How Acephate and Imidacloprid in LANSER GOLD deliver contact, stomach, and systemic absorption for comprehensive sucking pest control.

Contact action — rapid paralysis

Imidacloprid acts on contact, disrupting acetylcholinesterase in insect nerve tissue. Thrips, whiteflies, and aphids exposed to the spray deposit experience rapid paralysis and immediate cessation of feeding.

systemic action

When sucking pests ingest treated plant tissue, Imidacloprid delivers strong systemic insecticide activity against hidden feeders on leaf undersides and in dense canopy.

systemic absorption — Acephate

Acephate blocks nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, interrupting energy production. Susceptible pests lose the ability to feed and move normally — a powerful Acephate insecticide mechanism.

Resistance management benefits

Rotate LANSER GOLD with neonicotinoids, IGRs, and other MOA groups across the season to reduce selection pressure on thrips, whitefly, and aphid populations.
