Dosage & application guide

Clear, field-ready guidance for using Husi effectively.

Recommended dose

30 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 30 ml Husi, top up, and agitate until the solution is uniform before spraying.

Farmer tips for best results

Spray at flowering and fruit-development stages, cover the canopy well, and apply in calm morning or evening hours.
