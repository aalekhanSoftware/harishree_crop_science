Dosage & application guide

Clear, field-ready guidance for using MAGIC Fungicide effectively.

Recommended dose

30 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 30 ml MAGIC, top up, and agitate. The liquid formulation forms a uniform spray solution.

Farmer tips for best results

Spray at early disease signs, direct the spray to leaf sheaths and the lower canopy, and avoid spraying before heavy rain or strong wind.
