Chlorfenapyr 10% SC mode of action

How Chlorfenapyr in INTERPRID delivers cellular energy disruption for comprehensive thrips, mite, caterpillar, and borer control.

Contact action — rapid paralysis

Chlorfenapyr acts on contact, disrupting mitochondrial ATP production in pest cells. Thrips, mites, and caterpillars exposed to the spray deposit show reduced feeding followed by progressive mortality.

Energy production inhibition

When caterpillars, borers, and thrips ingest treated plant tissue, Chlorfenapyr disrupts cellular energy generation for effective control of hidden feeders.

Residual field protection

The SC formulation adheres to foliage and maintains contact insecticide activity on treated surfaces, protecting crops from pest resurgence between spray intervals.

Resistance management benefits

Rotate INTERPRID with IGRs, organophosphates, and other MOA groups across the season to reduce selection pressure on thrips, mite, and caterpillar populations.

Application method

Step 2 — Measure 30 ml

Accurately Measure 30 ml of INTERPRID for one knapsack pump. Use a graduated measure for consistent dosing.

Storage instructions

Store INTERPRID in the original sealed container in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place away from direct sunlight, food, feed, seed, and children. Keep containers upright and locked. Do not store near heat sources or open flames. Shelf life is approximately 24 months from date of manufacture when stored properly. Triple-rinse empty bottles before disposal as per local pesticide container management rules.
