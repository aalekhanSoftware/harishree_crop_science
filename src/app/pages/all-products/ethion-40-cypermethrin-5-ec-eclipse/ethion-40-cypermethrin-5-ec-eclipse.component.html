Ethion 40% + Cypermethrin 5% EC dose

Clear, field-ready guidance for using ECLIPSE effectively.

Recommended dose

25 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 25 ml ECLIPSE, top up, and agitate. The EC formulation mixes cleanly for uniform spray coverage.

Best spray timing

Apply at early pest infestation or ETL during calm morning or evening hours with full canopy coverage for maximum knockdown.

Ethion 40% + Cypermethrin 5% EC mode of action

How Ethion and Cypermethrin work together in ECLIPSE for comprehensive pest control.

Role of Ethion

Ethion (organophosphate, IRAC Group 1B) inhibits acetylcholinesterase in insect nerve tissue, causing paralysis through stomach and contact action on feeding larvae and exposed pests.

Role of Cypermethrin

Cypermethrin (synthetic pyrethroid, IRAC Group 3A) disrupts sodium channels for rapid knockdown and contact action against exposed pests on leaf surfaces.

Synergistic effect

The combination provides complementary modes of action — fast visible knockdown from Cypermethrin plus sustained stomach and contact activity from Ethion on mixed pest complexes.

Pest control mechanism

Dual nervous system disruption through two IRAC groups reduces selection pressure and improves control of pests that may show tolerance to single-action products.

Ethion 40% + Cypermethrin 5% EC for cotton

ECLIPSE is a leading dual-action choice for cotton pest management in India. Apply at 25 ml per pump when American bollworm, pink bollworm, spotted bollworm, jassids, aphids, or whiteflies reach economic threshold during square and boll stages. Cypermethrin delivers fast knockdown on exposed larvae while Ethion provides stomach action on feeding bollworms. Scout fields regularly and rotate with other IRAC groups for sustainable resistance management.

Ethion 40% + Cypermethrin 5% EC for chilli

In chilli, ECLIPSE controls thrips, aphids, fruit borers, and caterpillars that damage pods and reduce export quality. Spray at early infestation with 25 ml per pump, ensuring coverage of flowers and leaf undersides where thrips congregate. Timely application at vegetative and fruit-set stages protects pod colour, size, and marketable yield.

Ethion 40% + Cypermethrin 5% EC for vegetables

ECLIPSE fits tomato, brinjal, okra, cabbage, cauliflower, and other vegetable crops facing mixed chewing and sucking pest pressure. Use 25 ml per pump at early larval stage for fruit borers and caterpillars, or when aphids, whiteflies, and thrips reach ETL. Full canopy spray with underside coverage maximizes contact and stomach action for cleaner, market-ready produce.

Compatibility & tank mix

ECLIPSE is generally compatible with commonly used insecticides and fungicides when tank-mixed for integrated crop protection programs. Always perform a jar test before mixing to confirm physical compatibility. Avoid alkaline mixes that may degrade active ingredients. Consult your local Harishree dealer or agronomist for crop-specific tank-mix recommendations.

Resistance management

Dual active ingredients in ECLIPSE — Ethion (IRAC Group 1B) and Cypermethrin (IRAC Group 3A) — provide two distinct modes of action in a single spray, reducing selection pressure compared to single-action products. For sustainable pest management, rotate ECLIPSE with insecticides from other IRAC groups (e.g., IGRs, diamides, neonicotinoids) across the season and avoid repeated applications of the same chemistry class.
