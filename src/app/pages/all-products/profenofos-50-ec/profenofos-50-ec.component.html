Profenofos 50% EC (marketed as EXTRA PROFEX) is a premium broad-spectrum organophosphate insecticide engineered for powerful control of bollworms, whiteflies, aphids, thrips, jassids, and caterpillars. Clear, field-ready guidance for using EXTRA PROFEX effectively.

Recommended dose

30 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 30 ml EXTRA PROFEX, top up, and agitate. The EC formulation mixes cleanly without clogging.

Best spray timing

Apply at early pest infestation or ETL during calm morning or evening hours with full canopy coverage.
