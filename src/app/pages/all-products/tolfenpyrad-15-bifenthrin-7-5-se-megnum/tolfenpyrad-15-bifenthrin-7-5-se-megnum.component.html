Dual active ingredient technology

Tolfenpyrad 15% — mitochondrial respiration inhibitor

Tolfenpyrad (IRAC Group 21A) interrupts energy production at mitochondrial Complex I, stopping pest feeding rapidly. This Tolfenpyrad insecticide mechanism is especially effective against sucking pests and supports control of resistant pest populations when rotated within integrated pest management programs. Benefits include quick pest suppression, reduced crop damage from active feeders, and effective activity against thrips, whiteflies, aphids, and allied sap feeders.

Bifenthrin 7.5% — contact, stomach & residual action

Bifenthrin (IRAC Group 3A) delivers immediate impact through contact action — rapid paralysis of exposed pests — and stomach action when insects ingest treated tissue. The Bifenthrin insecticide component provides extended field protection through residual activity on foliage, making MEGNUM a true fast knockdown insecticide with lasting crop protection between spray intervals.

Tolfenpyrad 15% + Bifenthrin 7.5% SE mode of action

How Tolfenpyrad and Bifenthrin in MEGNUM deliver contact, stomach, and respiratory inhibition for comprehensive sucking pest control.

Contact action — rapid paralysis

Bifenthrin acts on contact, disrupting sodium channels in insect nerve tissue. Thrips, whiteflies, and aphids exposed to the spray deposit experience rapid paralysis and immediate cessation of feeding.

Stomach poison action

When sucking pests ingest treated plant tissue, Bifenthrin delivers strong stomach poison insecticide activity against hidden feeders on leaf undersides and in dense canopy.

Respiratory inhibition — Tolfenpyrad

Tolfenpyrad blocks mitochondrial Complex I, interrupting energy production. Susceptible pests lose the ability to feed and move normally — a powerful Tolfenpyrad insecticide mechanism.

Resistance management benefits

Rotate MEGNUM with neonicotinoids, IGRs, and other MOA groups across the season to reduce selection pressure on thrips, whitefly, and aphid populations.

Application method

Step-by-step guide for applying MEGNUM ({{ productTitle }}) in the field.

Step 1 — Prepare clean water

Half-fill a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer with clean water before adding the insecticide for safe, even mixing.

Step 2 — Measure 25 ml

Accurately measure 25 ml of MEGNUM for one knapsack pump. Use a graduated measure for consistent dosing.

Step 3 — Mix thoroughly

Storage instructions

Store MEGNUM in the original sealed container in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place away from direct sunlight, food, feed, seed, and children. Keep containers upright and locked. Triple-rinse empty bottles before disposal as per local pesticide container management rules.
