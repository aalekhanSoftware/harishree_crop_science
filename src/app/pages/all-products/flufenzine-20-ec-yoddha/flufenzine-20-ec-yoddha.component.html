FLUFENZINE 20% EC uses

Key agricultural applications of YODDHA across Indian farming systems.

Flufenzine application

Foliar spray at 25 ml per pump in a 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Apply at early mite infestation or economic threshold with full canopy and underside coverage.

Flufenzine benefits

Contact activity plus mite growth regulation disrupts feeding, moulting, and reproductive cycles for sustained suppression of red spider mites, yellow mites, and broad mites.

Crop protection solution

Trusted EC miticide for chilli red spider mites, cotton mite outbreaks, brinjal yellow mites, and mixed mite complexes on vegetables and horticulture crops across India.

FLUFENZINE 20% EC dose

Clear, field-ready guidance for using YODDHA effectively.

Recommended dose

25 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 25 ml YODDHA, top up, and agitate. The EC formulation emulsifies easily for uniform spray coverage on leaf surfaces.

Best spray timing

Apply at early mite infestation or ETL during calm morning or evening hours with emphasis on leaf undersides where mites colonize.

FLUFENZINE 20% EC mode of action

How Flufenzine in YODDHA delivers contact knockdown and mite growth regulation for comprehensive acaricidal control.

Contact activity on mites

Flufenzine forms an even EC deposit on foliage. Mites contacting treated surfaces absorb the active ingredient, disrupting feeding and movement.

Mite growth regulation

Flufenzine interferes with mite development pathways, affecting moulting and growth so nymphs fail to reach reproductive adulthood.

Reproductive cycle disruption

By impacting egg viability and nymph survival, YODDHA breaks the mite reproductive cycle and prevents rapid population explosions during hot dry periods.

Resistance management benefits

Rotate YODDHA with miticides and insecticides from other mode-of-action groups across the season to reduce selection pressure on mite populations.

YODDHA for chilli

In chilli, YODDHA controls red spider mites and yellow mites that cause bronzing, leaf distortion, and pod quality loss. Spray at early infestation with 25 ml per pump, ensuring coverage of leaf undersides where mites feed. Timely application at vegetative and fruit-set stages protects foliage colour, pod size, and marketable yield.

YODDHA for cotton

YODDHA fits cotton programs facing red spider mite pressure during square, flowering, and boll development stages. Use 25 ml per pump at early infestation with full canopy spray and underside coverage. Long residual EC activity helps maintain cleaner foliage and healthier plants throughout the season.

YODDHA for vegetables

Across brinjal, tomato, okra, cucurbits, and mixed vegetable crops, YODDHA controls red spider mites, yellow mites, and two-spotted spider mite outbreaks. Use 25 ml per pump at early mite colonization or ETL with full canopy coverage. Integrate with scouting and rotation across MOA groups for sustainable vegetable crop protection throughout the growing season.

YODDHA for red spider mite control

YODDHA delivers dependable control of red spider mites (Tetranychus species) on chilli, cotton, brinjal, tomato, and vegetable crops. Apply at economic threshold with 25 ml per pump and thorough underside coverage. Contact activity plus growth regulation suppresses eggs, nymphs, and adults for sustained mite population reduction between spray intervals.

FLUFENZINE 20% EC for yellow mite control

FLUFENZINE 20% EC in YODDHA provides strong activity against yellow mites (Polyphagotarsonemus latus) on chilli and brinjal when sprayed early with emphasis on leaf undersides. Timely application at first signs of leaf curling and chlorosis helps restore photosynthetic capacity and supports healthier harvest quality.
