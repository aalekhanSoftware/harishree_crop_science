SC · Dual action · IGR + Oxadiazine · Caterpillar & borer control

Novaluron 5.25% + Indoxacarb 4.5% w/w SC (marketed as TURBO) is a premium dual-action suspension concentrate insecticide engineered for powerful lepidopteran control. The combination pairs an insect growth regulator with a modern oxadiazine to stop caterpillar feeding fast and disrupt larval moulting for extended crop protection in cotton, chilli, tomato, cole crops, soybean, pulses, and vegetables.

Recommended dose

20 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 20 ml TURBO, top up, and agitate. The SC formulation mixes cleanly without clogging.

Best spray timing

Apply at early larval infestation or ETL during calm morning or evening hours with full canopy coverage.
