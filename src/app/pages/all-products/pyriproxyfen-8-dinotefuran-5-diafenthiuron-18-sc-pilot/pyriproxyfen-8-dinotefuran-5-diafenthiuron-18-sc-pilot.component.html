Triple action technology in PILOT

Three proven actives — each targeting a different stage and mode of sucking pest control.

Pyriproxyfen 8% — Insect Growth Regulator

Pyriproxyfen mimics juvenile hormone to prevent whitefly and aphid eggs from hatching and stops nymphs from completing moults. This IGR action breaks pest lifecycles at source, reducing rapid population resurgence between spray intervals on treated foliage.

Dinotefuran 5% — Systemic knockdown

Dinotefuran is a neonicotinoid that moves systemically within plant tissues, disrupting the nervous system of hidden aphids, whitefly, thrips, and jassids feeding on new growth. Fast feeding cessation reduces honeydew, sooty mould, and virus transmission risk quickly after application.

Diafenthiuron 18% — Contact residual control

Diafenthiuron provides strong contact and translaminar activity on foliage, maintaining long residual control against exposed and resistant sucking pest populations. Its thiourea chemistry complements the IGR and systemic actives for season-long protection.

Recommended dose — 30 ml per pump

Clear, field-ready guidance for using PILOT effectively against sucking pests.

Recommended dose

30 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label and with local agronomic advisory.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 30 ml PILOT, top up, and agitate. The SC formulation disperses easily for uniform spray coverage on leaf surfaces and undersides.

Best spray timing

Apply at early sucking pest infestation or economic threshold during calm morning or evening hours. Emphasize leaf undersides where whitefly, aphids, thrips, and jassids feed and colonize.

Application method

Step-by-step field guidance for preparing and applying PILOT.

Step 1 — Prepare PPE

Wear gloves, goggles, and a mask. Keep children and animals away from the mixing area before handling concentrate.

Step 2 — Measure 30 ml

Accurately measure 30 ml of PILOT for a standard 15-litre knapsack pump. Use a graduated measure for consistent dosing.

Step 3 — Mix and spray

Half-fill tank with water, add product, top up, and agitate. Spray evenly in calm weather with full canopy and underside coverage.

Step 4 — Post-spray care

Wash exposed skin, clean equipment, store leftover product safely, and observe re-entry interval before field work resumes.

Storage instructions

Store PILOT in the original sealed container in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place away from direct sunlight, food, feed, seed, and children. Keep containers upright and locked. Do not store near heat sources or open flames. Shelf life is approximately 24 months from date of manufacture when stored properly. Triple-rinse empty bottles before disposal as per local pesticide container management rules.

Mode of action — systemic, contact, and IGR
