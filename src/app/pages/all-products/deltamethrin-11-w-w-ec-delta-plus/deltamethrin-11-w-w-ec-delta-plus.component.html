Deltamethrin 11% w/w EC mode of action

How Deltamethrin in DELTA PLUS delivers contact and stomach action for comprehensive caterpillar and borer control.

Contact action — rapid paralysis

Deltamethrin acts on contact, disrupting sodium channels in insect nerve tissue. Pests exposed to the spray deposit experience rapid paralysis and immediate cessation of feeding.

Stomach poison action

When caterpillars, borers, and bollworms ingest treated plant tissue, Deltamethrin delivers strong stomach poison insecticide activity against hidden feeders.

Residual field protection

The EC formulation adheres to foliage and maintains contact insecticide activity on treated surfaces, protecting crops from pest resurgence between spray intervals.

Resistance management benefits

Rotate DELTA PLUS with IGRs, organophosphates, and other MOA groups across the season to reduce selection pressure on bollworm and caterpillar populations.

Application method

Step-by-step guide for applying DELTA PLUS ({{ productTitle }}) in the field.

Step 1 — Prepare clean water

Half-fill a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer with clean water before adding the insecticide for safe, even mixing.

Step 2 — Measure 10 ml

Accurately measure 10 ml of DELTA PLUS for one knapsack pump. Use a graduated measure for consistent dosing.

Step 3 — Mix thoroughly

Add product to water, top up the tank, and agitate. The EC formulation emulsifies easily into a uniform spray mix.

Step 4 — Spray uniformly

Apply in calm weather. Cover both leaf surfaces and undersides where caterpillars, borers, and bollworms feed.

Storage instructions

Store DELTA PLUS in the original sealed container in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place away from direct sunlight, food, feed, seed, and children. Keep containers upright and locked. Do not store near heat sources or open flames. Shelf life is approximately 24 months from date of manufacture when stored properly. Triple-rinse empty bottles before disposal as per local pesticide container management rules.
