Fipronil 2.92% EC dose

Clear, field-ready guidance for using CHILLI H effectively.

Recommended dose

20 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 20 ml CHILLI H, top up, and agitate. The EC formulation mixes cleanly for uniform spray coverage.

Best spray timing

Apply at early pest infestation or ETL during calm morning or evening hours with full canopy coverage for maximum residual protection.

Fipronil 2.92% EC mode of action

How Fipronil works in CHILLI H for comprehensive pest control through GABA receptor disruption.

GABA receptor disruption

Fipronil (IRAC Group 2B) blocks GABA-gated chloride channels in insect nerve tissue, preventing normal nerve inhibition and causing hyperexcitation, paralysis, and death.

Pest nervous system impact

By disrupting chloride channel function, fipronil affects both central and peripheral nervous systems, providing broad-spectrum control of chewing and sucking pests.

Feeding control

Contact and stomach action stop feeding damage from stem borers, thrips, white grubs, and root feeders during critical crop growth stages.

Long residual performance

The EC formulation maintains active deposit on foliage and soil surfaces, continuing to protect crops from newly arriving pests between spray intervals.

Fipronil 2.92% EC for rice

CHILLI H is a leading choice for rice pest management in India. Apply at 20 ml per pump when yellow stem borer, pink stem borer, leaf folder, or BPH reach economic threshold during tillering and panicle initiation. Fipronil delivers contact and stomach action on feeding larvae while maintaining long residual protection on tillers and foliage. Scout fields regularly and rotate with other IRAC groups for sustainable resistance management.

Fipronil 2.92% EC for chilli

In chilli, CHILLI H controls thrips, aphids, and soil insects that damage pods and reduce export quality. Spray at early infestation with 20 ml per pump, ensuring coverage of flowers and leaf undersides where thrips congregate. Timely application at vegetative and fruit-set stages protects pod colour, size, and marketable yield.

Fipronil 2.92% EC for cotton

CHILLI H fits cotton programs facing jassids, aphids, thrips, and soil pest pressure. Use 20 ml per pump at early infestation during vegetative and boll development stages. Full canopy spray with underside coverage maximizes contact and stomach action for cleaner foliage and healthier plants throughout the season.

Fipronil 2.92% EC for sugarcane

In sugarcane, CHILLI H manages stem borer, white grub, and termite infestations that reduce cane weight and juice quality. Apply at 20 ml per pump when early borer or soil pest damage is detected. Proper coverage of stems and base of plants supports stand vigour and productivity through the crop cycle.

Compatibility & tank mix

CHILLI H is generally compatible with commonly used insecticides and fungicides when tank-mixed for integrated crop protection programs. Always perform a jar test before mixing to confirm physical compatibility. Avoid alkaline mixes that may degrade active ingredients. Consult your local Harishree dealer or agronomist for crop-specific tank-mix recommendations.

Resistance management

Fipronil in CHILLI H (IRAC Group 2B) provides a distinct GABA receptor mode of action. For sustainable pest management, rotate CHILLI H with insecticides from other IRAC groups (e.g., IGRs, diamides, organophosphates, pyrethroids) across the season and avoid repeated applications of the same chemistry class.
