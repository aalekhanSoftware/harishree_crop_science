Dual active ingredient technology

Buprofezin 15% — insect growth regulator (IGR)

Buprofezin (IRAC Group 16) inhibits chitin biosynthesis in immature pest stages, preventing successful moulting and development. This buprofezin insecticide mechanism disrupts whitefly nymphs, planthopper nymphs, and aphid nymphs — reducing pest multiplication and resurgence. Benefits include long residual IGR activity, effective control of immature sucking pest populations, and reduced crop damage from developing pest generations between spray intervals.

Long residual effect

Buprofezin provides extended protection on treated foliage, maintaining IGR activity against newly hatching nymphs and supporting sustainable sucking pest management when rotated within integrated pest management programs.

ACEPHATE 50% — organophosphate insecticide

Acephate (IRAC Group 1B) inhibits acetylcholinesterase in insect nerve tissue, causing rapid nervous-system disruption and feeding cessation. This acephate insecticide delivers contact action, systemic absorption through plant tissue, and fast knockdown against whiteflies, brown planthoppers, aphids, jassids, thrips, and allied sap feeders. Benefits include quick visible results and broad-spectrum field performance on high-pressure sucking pest outbreaks.

Broad-spectrum control

Acephate provides effective activity on multiple sucking pest species in cotton, paddy, chilli, and vegetable crops — delivering the fast-acting component that complements buprofezin IGR lifecycle disruption in TOPAZ.

Buprofezin 15% + ACEPHATE 50% mode of action

How Acephate and Buprofezin in TOPAZ deliver contact, stomach, and systemic absorption for comprehensive sucking pest control.

Contact action — rapid knockdown

Acephate acts on contact, inhibiting acetylcholinesterase in insect nerve tissue. Whiteflies, planthoppers, jassids, and aphids exposed to the spray deposit experience rapid nervous-system disruption and immediate cessation of feeding damage.

Systemic action

When sucking pests ingest treated plant tissue, acephate delivers strong systemic insecticide activity against hidden feeders on leaf undersides and in dense canopy — protecting new shoots after foliar uptake.

Growth regulation — Buprofezin IGR

Buprofezin inhibits chitin biosynthesis in immature pest stages, preventing successful moulting. Whitefly nymphs, planthopper nymphs, and aphid nymphs fail to develop — reducing pest multiplication and resurgence between spray intervals.

Resistance management benefits

Rotate TOPAZ with neonicotinoids, pyrethroids, and other MOA groups across the season to reduce selection pressure on whitefly, planthopper, and jassid populations in integrated pest management programs.

Application method
