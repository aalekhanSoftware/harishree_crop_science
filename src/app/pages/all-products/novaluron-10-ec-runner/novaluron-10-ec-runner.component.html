NOVALURON 10% EC mode of action

How Novaluron in RUNNER delivers insect growth regulation for comprehensive caterpillar and borer control.

Chitin synthesis inhibition

Novaluron blocks chitin biosynthesis — the structural protein caterpillars need to form a new exoskeleton during moulting.

Larval moulting disruption

Affected instars fail to shed their old cuticle. Larvae become trapped in malformed exoskeletons and cannot reach reproductive adulthood.

Population cycle break

By stopping development at vulnerable instars, RUNNER breaks the caterpillar reproductive cycle and prevents rapid population build-up during peak borer seasons.

Resistance management benefits

Rotate RUNNER with contact insecticides and other MOA groups across the season to reduce selection pressure on caterpillar and borer populations.
