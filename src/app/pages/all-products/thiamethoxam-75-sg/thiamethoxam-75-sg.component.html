SG · Systemic · Broad-spectrum neonicotinoid insecticide

Thiamethoxam 75% SG (marketed as Thio-H 75 / Thio-H) is a premium, powerful systemic insecticide engineered for exceptional control of sucking pests. The high-concentration soluble granule (SG) formulation dissolves instantly, absorbs rapidly into plant tissues, and protects crops against aphids, jassids, hoppers, whiteflies, and thrips while promoting visibly greener, healthier growth.
