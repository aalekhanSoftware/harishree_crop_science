Emamectin Benzoate 1.5% + FIPRONIL 3.5% SC dose

Clear, field-ready guidance for using PRO 5G effectively.

Recommended dose

25 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 25 ml PRO 5G, top up, and agitate. Shake the bottle first; the SC formulation mixes cleanly for uniform spray coverage.

Best spray timing

Apply at early pest infestation or ETL during calm morning or evening hours with full canopy coverage for maximum residual protection.

Emamectin Benzoate 1.5% + FIPRONIL 3.5% SC mode of action

How Emamectin and Fipronil work together in PRO 5G for comprehensive dual-action pest control.

Role of Emamectin Benzoate

Emamectin (IRAC Group 6) activates glutamate-gated chloride channels, causing paralysis in lepidopteran larvae and providing translaminar movement into leaf tissue.

Role of Fipronil

Fipronil (IRAC Group 2B) blocks GABA-gated chloride channels, disrupting nerve signals in both chewing and sucking pests through contact and stomach action.

Synergistic effect

Combined avermectin and phenylpyrazole chemistry attacks two biochemical pathways, improving control of mixed pest complexes in a single spray.

Resistance management benefits

Dual MOA reduces selection pressure on any single pathway. Rotate PRO 5G with other IRAC groups across the season for sustainable pest management.

Compatibility & tank mix

PRO 5G is generally compatible with commonly used insecticides and fungicides when tank-mixed for integrated crop protection programs. Always perform a jar test before mixing to confirm physical compatibility. Avoid alkaline mixes that may degrade active ingredients. Consult your local Harishree dealer or agronomist for crop-specific tank-mix recommendations.

Resistance management

Dual active ingredients in PRO 5G (IRAC Groups 6 + 2B) provide complementary modes of action. For sustainable pest management, rotate PRO 5G with insecticides from other IRAC groups (e.g., IGRs, diamides, organophosphates) across the season and avoid repeated applications of the same chemistry class.
