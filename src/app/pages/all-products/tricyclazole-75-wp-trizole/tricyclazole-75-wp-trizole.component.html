Dosage & application guide

Clear, field-ready guidance for using TRIZOLE Fungicide effectively.

Recommended dose

10 gm per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-stage rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 10 gm TRIZOLE, top up, and agitate. The WP formulation forms a uniform spray suspension.

Farmer tips for best results

Spray at early leaf blast lesions and again at boot-leaf to panicle stage, cover the canopy well, and avoid spraying before heavy rain.
