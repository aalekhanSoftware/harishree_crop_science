Chlorantraniliprole 9.3% + Lambda Cyhalothrin 4.6% ZC dose

Clear, field-ready guidance for using AMPIGO effectively.

Recommended dose

10 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 10 ml AMPIGO, top up, and agitate. Shake the bottle first; the ZC formulation mixes cleanly for uniform spray coverage.

Best spray timing

Apply at early larval infestation or ETL during calm morning or evening hours with full canopy coverage for maximum residual protection.

Chlorantraniliprole 9.3% + Lambda Cyhalothrin 4.6% ZC mode of action

How Chlorantraniliprole and Lambda Cyhalothrin work together in AMPIGO for comprehensive dual-action pest control.

Role of Chlorantraniliprole

Chlorantraniliprole (IRAC Group 28) activates ryanodine receptors, depleting calcium in muscle cells and causing feeding cessation in caterpillars and borers with translaminar movement.

Role of Lambda Cyhalothrin

Lambda Cyhalothrin (IRAC Group 3A) modulates voltage-gated sodium channels, disrupting nerve signals for rapid knockdown through contact and stomach action.

Synergistic effect

Combined diamide and pyrethroid chemistry attacks two biochemical pathways, improving control of tough caterpillar and borer complexes in a single spray.

Resistance management benefits

Dual MOA (IRAC 28 + 3A) reduces selection pressure on any single pathway. Rotate AMPIGO with other IRAC groups across the season for sustainable pest management.

AMPIGO for cotton

AMPIGO fits cotton programs facing bollworm, Helicoverpa, and Spodoptera pressure. Use 10 ml per pump at early infestation during square, flowering, and boll development stages. Full canopy spray with underside coverage maximizes dual-action contact, stomach, and translaminar activity for cleaner bolls and healthier plants throughout the season.

AMPIGO for maize

In maize, AMPIGO is a leading choice for fall armyworm and stem borer management during vegetative and cob development stages. Spray at early larval infestation with 10 ml per pump, ensuring thorough coverage of the whorl and leaf folds where armyworm larvae hide. Timely application protects cob quality and supports better grain yield at harvest.

AMPIGO for chilli

In chilli, AMPIGO controls fruit borer and Spodoptera that damage pods and reduce export quality. Spray at early infestation with 10 ml per pump, ensuring coverage of flowers and leaf undersides where caterpillars feed. Timely application at vegetative and fruit-set stages protects pod colour, size, and marketable yield.

AMPIGO for vegetables

Across brinjal, okra, cabbage, cauliflower, tomato, and other vegetable crops, AMPIGO controls mixed caterpillar and borer pressure. Use 10 ml per pump at early larval stage or ETL with full canopy coverage. Integrate with scouting and rotation across IRAC groups for sustainable vegetable crop protection throughout the growing season.

AMPIGO for fall armyworm control

AMPIGO delivers fast knockdown and feeding cessation against fall armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda) in maize and other susceptible crops. Apply at early larval stage when pests first appear in the whorl, using 10 ml per pump with thorough coverage of leaf folds and growing points. The dual-action ZC formulation provides both immediate pest suppression and residual protection against newly hatched larvae between spray intervals.

Compatibility & tank mix

AMPIGO is generally compatible with commonly used insecticides and fungicides when tank-mixed for integrated crop protection programs. Always perform a jar test before mixing to confirm physical compatibility. Avoid alkaline mixes that may degrade active ingredients. Consult your local Harishree dealer or agronomist for crop-specific tank-mix recommendations.

Resistance management

Dual active ingredients in AMPIGO (IRAC Groups 28 + 3A) provide complementary modes of action. For sustainable pest management, rotate AMPIGO with insecticides from other IRAC groups (e.g., IGRs, oxadiazines, organophosphates) across the season and avoid repeated applications of the same chemistry class.
