Meta 35 Fungicide ({{ productTitle }}) is a premium systemic seed-treatment fungicide from HARISHREE CROP SCIENCE, trusted by farmers for clean, uniform crop establishment. Dosage & application guide

Clear, field-ready guidance for using Meta 35 Fungicide as a seed treatment.

Recommended dose

25 gm per pump for seed treatment. Confirm the crop-wise seed-treatment rate on the label.

Slurry preparation

Mix the measured Meta 35 with a little clean water to form a uniform slurry, then coat the seed thoroughly before sowing.

Farmer tips for best results

Treat clean, good-quality seed, ensure even coating, dry treated seed in shade, and sow promptly for clean, uniform establishment.
