Active ingredient technology

Acephate 75% SP — organophosphate chemistry

Acephate (IRAC Group 1B) is a proven organophosphate insecticide that inhibits acetylcholinesterase in insect nerve tissue, causing feeding disruption and nervous-system impact. This widely trusted chemistry delivers effective pest control against thrips, aphids, whiteflies, jassids, bollworms, and allied sucking and chewing pests across Indian farming systems.

Systemic action — absorbed by plant tissues

Acephate absorbs through foliage and translocates via xylem, protecting new shoots and unsprayed plant parts from inside out. Systemic movement ensures long-lasting activity against newly active pests between spray intervals, making HIL THENE a true systemic insecticide for dependable crop protection in cotton, chilli, paddy, tomato, brinjal, okra, and vegetable programs.

Contact action — quick pest knockdown

Acephate delivers rapid contact kill on pests directly exposed to the spray deposit. Thrips, whiteflies, aphids, and jassids experience immediate feeding cessation and nervous-system disruption — providing fast pest suppression when applied at economic threshold with thorough leaf surface and underside coverage at 25 gm per pump.

ACEPHATE 75% SP mode of action

How Acephate in HIL THENE delivers contact, systemic, and translaminar action for comprehensive thrips, aphid, whitefly, and bollworm control.

Contact action — rapid knockdown

Acephate acts on contact, inhibiting acetylcholinesterase in insect nerve tissue. Thrips, whiteflies, aphids, and jassids exposed to the spray deposit experience rapid nervous-system disruption and immediate cessation of feeding.

Systemic activity

When pests ingest treated plant tissue, acephate delivers strong systemic insecticide activity against hidden feeders on leaf undersides and in dense canopy — protecting the entire crop from inside out.

Feeding disruption

Acephate disrupts normal nerve signalling through acetylcholinesterase inhibition. Susceptible pests lose the ability to feed and move normally — a powerful organophosphate insecticide mechanism.

Long residual protection

Systemic acephate activity within plant tissue maintains protection against newly active pests between spray intervals. Rotate HIL THENE with neonicotinoids, IGRs, and other MOA groups across the season.
