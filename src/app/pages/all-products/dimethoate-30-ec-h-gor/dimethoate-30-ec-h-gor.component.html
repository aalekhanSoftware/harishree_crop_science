Dimethoate 30% EC uses

Key agricultural applications of H GOR across Indian farming systems.

Dimethoate application

Foliar spray at 30 ml per pump in a 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Apply at early pest infestation or economic threshold with full canopy coverage including leaf undersides.

Dimethoate benefits

Systemic absorption protects new shoots and leaves. Combined contact action delivers rapid knockdown of aphids, jassids, whiteflies, and thrips on treated foliage.

Crop protection solution

Trusted organophosphate insecticide for cotton jassids, chilli thrips, vegetable aphids, and pulse crop sucking pest complexes across India.

Dimethoate 30% EC dose

Clear, field-ready guidance for using H GOR effectively.

Recommended dose

30 ml per pump in a standard 15-litre knapsack sprayer. Confirm crop-wise rate on the label.

Mixing instructions

Half-fill with water, add 30 ml H GOR, top up, and agitate. The EC formulation mixes cleanly for uniform systemic uptake.

Best spray timing

Apply at early pest infestation or ETL during calm morning or evening hours with full canopy coverage for maximum absorption.
