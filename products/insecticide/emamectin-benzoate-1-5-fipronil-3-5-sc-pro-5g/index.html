What is Emamectin Benzoate 1.5% + FIPRONIL 3.5% SC?

Emamectin Benzoate 1.5% + FIPRONIL 3.5% SC (PRO 5G) is a dual-action suspension concentrate insecticide combining avermectin and phenylpyrazole chemistry for broad-spectrum control of caterpillars, bollworms, borers, thrips, aphids, and whiteflies through contact, stomach, and translaminar activity. Recommended dose: 25 ml per pump.

How does Emamectin Benzoate 1.5% + FIPRONIL 3.5% SC work?

Dual-action chemistry — Emamectin and Fipronil disrupt insect nervous systems through contact, stomach, and translaminar activity.

  1. 1

    Dual mode of action

    PRO 5G combines Emamectin Benzoate (IRAC Group 6) and Fipronil (IRAC Group 2B) for complementary nervous system disruption in one application.

  2. 2

    Contact action

    Pests contacting treated foliage absorb active ingredients through the cuticle, delivering rapid knockdown on exposed larvae and sucking pests.

  3. 3

    Stomach action

    Feeding caterpillars and borers ingest treated plant tissue, receiving lethal doses through the digestive tract.

  4. 4

    Translaminar activity

    Emamectin moves into leaf tissue, reaching pests sheltered on the underside or within partially consumed leaves.

  5. 5

    Nervous system disruption

    Emamectin activates GluCl channels while Fipronil blocks GABA receptors, causing paralysis and feeding cessation.

  6. 6

    Resistance management benefits

    Dual chemistry reduces selection pressure on a single MOA and supports sustainable rotation within IPM programs.

How Emamectin and Fipronil work together in PRO 5G for comprehensive dual-action pest control.

Emamectin (IRAC Group 6) activates glutamate-gated chloride channels, causing paralysis in lepidopteran larvae and providing translaminar movement into leaf tissue.
