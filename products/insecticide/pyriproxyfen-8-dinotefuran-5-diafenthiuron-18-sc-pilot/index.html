How does PILOT triple action technology work?

Systemic, contact, and insect growth regulation — Pyriproxyfen, Dinotefuran, and Diafenthiuron work together for comprehensive sucking pest control.

  1. 1

    SC deposit on foliage

    On application, PILOT forms an even suspension concentrate film on leaf surfaces. Sucking pests ingest or contact treated foliage, absorbing all three active ingredients into their system.

  2. 2

    Pyriproxyfen — IGR lifecycle disruption

    Pyriproxyfen mimics juvenile hormone, preventing whitefly and aphid eggs from hatching and stopping nymphs from completing moults — breaking the pest reproductive cycle at source.

  3. 3

    Dinotefuran — systemic knockdown

    Dinotefuran is absorbed and translocated within plant tissues, delivering fast nervous-system disruption to hidden aphids, whitefly, thrips, and jassids feeding on new growth.

  4. 4

    Diafenthiuron — contact residual control

    Diafenthiuron provides strong contact and translaminar activity on foliage, maintaining long residual control against exposed and resistant sucking pest populations.

  5. 5

    Triple-action population suppression

    Combined IGR, systemic, and contact effects suppress whitefly, aphid, thrips, and jassid populations across multiple life stages — reducing rapid resurgence between spray intervals.

  6. 6

    Crop protection benefits

    Reduced sap-feeding preserves leaf integrity, fruit quality, and plant vigour — supporting healthier cotton, brinjal, chilli, tomato, and vegetable crops through harvest.

Triple action technology in PILOT

Three proven actives — each targeting a different stage and mode of sucking pest control.

Pyriproxyfen 8% — Insect Growth Regulator

Pyriproxyfen mimics juvenile hormone to prevent whitefly and aphid eggs from hatching and stops nymphs from completing moults. This IGR action breaks pest lifecycles at source, reducing rapid population resurgence between spray intervals on treated foliage.

Dinotefuran is a neonicotinoid that moves systemically within plant tissues, disrupting the nervous system of hidden aphids, whitefly, thrips, and jassids feeding on new growth. Fast feeding cessation reduces honeydew, sooty mould, and virus transmission risk quickly after application.

Diafenthiuron 18% — Contact residual control

Diafenthiuron provides strong contact and translaminar activity on foliage, maintaining long residual control against exposed and resistant sucking pest populations. Its thiourea chemistry complements the IGR and systemic actives for season-long protection.
