Mode of action

How Glufosinate ammonium 13.5% SL (GLUFO 13) works to suppress emerged weeds after foliar uptake.

  1. 1

    Foliar absorption

    GLUFO 13 (Glufosinate ammonium 13.5% SL) is absorbed by green tissues after spray deposition on actively growing weed leaves.

  2. 2

    Enzyme inhibition

    The active ingredient inhibits glutamine synthetase in susceptible plants after uptake, blocking normal nitrogen metabolism.

  3. 3

    Ammonia accumulation

    Toxic ammonia accumulation disrupts photosynthesis and cellular function, leading to rapid loss of weed vigour.

  4. 4

    Weed desiccation

    Treated weeds gradually lose vigour and dry down, reducing competition for light, moisture, and nutrients in treated zones.

Usage & safety guidelines

Practical habits that keep spraying effective and people protected when using GLUFO 13.

5 gm accurate dose PPE & drift control

Usage best practices

Mix clean, spray calm, scout again after a few days.

  • 1

    Wear proper PPE

    Always wear gloves, protective clothing, a fitted mask, and eye protection when handling GLUFO 13 or spray mix.

  • 2

    Accurate 5 gm dose

    Adhere precisely to the 5 gm per pump dosage and calibrate sprayer output for uniform coverage and dose accuracy.

  • 3

    Spray for real coverage

    Use steady spray technique with proper nozzle and pressure. Good coverage improves effectiveness on actively growing weeds.

Safety & stewardship

Respect the label hazard panel and local disposal rules.

  • 4

    Avoid drift

    Spray during calm weather and protect nearby desirable plants from contact. GLUFO 13 is non-selective on green tissue.

  • 5

    Directed spray only

    Do not spray directly on green crop foliage or stems unless the label specifically allows it for that crop.

  • 6

    Storage and disposal

    Store sealed away from children and feed. Triple-rinse containers and dispose according to local agricultural regulations.
