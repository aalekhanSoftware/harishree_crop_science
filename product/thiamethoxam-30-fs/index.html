Mode of action

How Thiamethoxam 30% FS works during early-stage crop development.

  1. 1

    Targets nicotinic acetylcholine receptors

    Thiamethoxam affects receptor function in insect nerves, causing nervous disruption that reduces feeding and active movement.

  2. 2

    Systemic seedling-phase protection

    As seeds germinate, treated seeds support early protection of young plants during vulnerable growth stages.

  3. 3

    Multi-path field activity

    The product contributes systemic, contact and stomach action profile in practical integrated pest management schedules.

  4. 4

    Best results with timely pre-sowing treatment

    Uniform seed coating before sowing helps improve protection consistency and supports stronger early crop stand.

Usage & safety guidelines

Follow a disciplined seed-treatment process for reliable outcomes and safe handling.

Seed treatment discipline PPE and stewardship

Usage best practices

Measure right, coat uniformly, and sow in planned window.

  • 1

    Wear PPE while treating seed

    Use gloves, mask and basic protective clothing when measuring and coating seeds.

  • 2

    Measure exact dose before mixing

    Use recommended dose of 15 ml/pump and avoid over-concentration during treatment.

  • 3

    Ensure uniform coating

    Use a seed treater or manual mixing with sticking agent to achieve even seed coverage.

Safety and responsibility

Keep treated seed for sowing only and follow handling protocol.

  • 4

    Treat seeds before sowing window

    Plan treatment at least 1-2 hours before sowing for practical handling and drying.

  • 5

    Dry treated seed in shade only

    Avoid direct sunlight after treatment; allow seeds to dry under shade conditions.

  • 6

    Keep treated seeds out of food chain

    Never use treated seeds for food, feed, or oil extraction and store them separately.
