Mode of action

How Thiamethoxam 75% SG (Thio-H 75) works from the inside out to protect your crops.

  1. 1

    Rapid dissolution and uptake

    Thiamethoxam 75% SG dissolves instantly in water and is quickly taken up by roots and foliage of the treated crop.

  2. 2

    Systemic distribution

    The active ingredient moves through the xylem, distributing uniformly to all plant parts including sensitive new growth.

  3. 3

    Nervous system disruption

    Acts on the central nervous system of insects by interfering with nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, paralysing pests.

  4. 4

    Feeding cessation

    Affected insects rapidly stop feeding and die, immediately halting crop damage and protecting yield potential.

Usage & safety guidelines

Practical habits that keep spraying effective and people protected when using Thio-H 75.

5 gm accurate dose PPE & environment

Usage best practices

Mix clean, spray calm, scout again after a few days.

  • 1

    Wear proper PPE

    Always wear gloves, protective clothing, a fitted mask, and eye protection when handling granules or spray mix.

  • 2

    Accurate 5 gm dose

    Adhere precisely to the 5 gm per pump dosage to prevent resistance build-up and ensure crop safety.

  • 3

    Spray for real coverage

    Use steady spray technique to reach leaf undersides. Good coverage improves effectiveness on thrips and sucking pests.

Safety & stewardship

Respect the label hazard panel and local disposal rules.

  • 4

    Environmental care

    Avoid spraying near water bodies. Do not spray during active bee foraging times to protect pollinators.

  • 5

    Equipment hygiene

    Thoroughly wash the sprayer immediately after use. Empty containers must be triple-rinsed and disposed of safely.

  • 6

    Storage and first aid

    Store in a cool, dry place away from children and animal feed. Seek medical help immediately if accidental ingestion occurs.
